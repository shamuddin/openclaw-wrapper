# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want OpenClaw Wrapper to be a welcoming, respectful, and technically generous project.

People participating in the project are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent while still giving honest feedback
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people
- help create an environment where newcomers can ask questions safely
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- abusive or demeaning language
- harassment or threats
- discriminatory remarks
- deliberate disruption of discussion or collaboration
- publishing someone else's private information without permission

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces such as issues, pull requests, discussions, chats, reviews, and other community interactions tied to the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take action they believe is appropriate to protect the community, including warning, moderation, content removal, temporary restriction, or removal from participation.

## Reporting

If someone is making the project unsafe or hostile, report it privately to the maintainers rather than escalating publicly.

## Maintainer Expectations

Maintainers should apply this code of conduct consistently, act in good faith, and prioritize community safety over winning arguments.
